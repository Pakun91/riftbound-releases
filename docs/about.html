Source

All tournament results and decklists are pulled from riftools.app, which aggregates results from several tournament-organizer platforms (Battlefy, TopDeck, CircleTCG, and others). This app only stores Vendetta Constructed results — a tournament qualifies either because riftools tags it that way, or because its decks use Legends already confirmed as part of the Vendetta pool (some organizers upload real Vendetta results under a different/wrong meta label).
